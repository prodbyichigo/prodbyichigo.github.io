# Caustic Wax

<img src="../assets/bss/wax/caustic_wax.png" alt="Caustic Wax" class="small">

> "A glowing hunk of beeswax. Has a 25% chance to GREATLY improve a Beequip - but a 75% chance to DESTROY it!"

**Caustic Wax** is a rare consumable inventory item and wax type added in the **2021-12-26 update**. It can be applied to a **Beequip** to potentially boost its stats — but at significant risk.

---

## Overview

Upon use, Caustic Wax rolls one of two outcomes:

| Outcome | Probability | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Success | 25% | **Beequip** stats are **greatly improved.** |
| Failure | 75% | **Beequip** is **permanently destroyed.** |

Caustic Wax has an **Upgrade Value of 4**, making it the highest-impact wax in the game. It is also the **only wax capable of destroying Beequips**.

> **Note:** Caustic Wax cannot be used on permanent *Beequips*.

---

## How to Obtain

### Crafting

Craft at the **Blender** using:

- 5× Hard Wax
- 5× Enzymes
- 25× Neonberries
- 5,252× Royal Jellies

### Drops

- Rare drop from **Legendary and Mythic Puffshrooms**
- Very rare drop from **Sticker Sprouts**
- Drop from a **level 17 Snowbear**

...and so on.
